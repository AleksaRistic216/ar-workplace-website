# Launch checklist — Show HN and Product Hunt

Both are one-shot. A Show HN that lands at 40 points is not something you get to retry in a month
with better copy, and the front page is decided in the first ninety minutes.

## Do not launch until

- [ ] **The trial question is decided.** With no trial and no free tier, "I can't try it" will be
      the top comment, and every reply below it is you defending the business model instead of
      talking about the product. Either ship a trial or have a genuinely good answer ready.
- [ ] **macOS has shipped, or the post does not mention it.** A third of HN is on macOS. "Coming
      soon" in a launch post reads as "not for you" to those readers.
- [ ] The `/vs` and `/guides` pages are live — that is where the traffic that does not buy today
      goes, and where it comes back from later.
- [ ] `/changelog` shows recent activity. It is the single best trust signal on the site.
- [ ] Downloads work, on both platforms, from a clean machine. Test it on one.
- [ ] The licence dialog is not the first thing a curious person hits with no explanation.

## Show HN

**Title.** `Show HN: CPT – a terminal that behaves identically on Windows and Linux`
Under 80 characters, no superlatives, no exclamation mark. The rules are at
<https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html> — read them again on the day.

**First comment, posted by you, immediately.** This matters more than the title. Cover:

- Why you built it — the actual reason, in two sentences.
- What it does that the free ones do not, stated narrowly enough to be checkable.
- What it does *not* do: no macOS yet, no free tier, closed source, crypto-only payment. Say it all
  in your own post. Getting there first is worth several hours of goodwill.
- The price, plainly. Never make anyone hunt for it.

**Timing.** Tuesday to Thursday, 08:00–10:00 US Eastern. Not a Friday, not a holiday week.

**For the next six hours, you are on HN and nothing else.** Reply to every comment, including the
hostile ones, especially the hostile ones. Never argue about the price. Never say "great question".

**Never:** ask anyone to upvote, post the link anywhere while it is live, or use a second account.
HN detects voting rings and the penalty is the account and the domain.

## Product Hunt

Lower value for this audience than HN, and worth doing only once macOS ships.

- [ ] Gallery: the clips from `scripts/capture-clips.sh`, square format first.
- [ ] Tagline under 60 characters.
- [ ] First comment written before launch, same honesty rules as HN.
- [ ] Launch 00:01 PT, and be awake for it.

## The day after

- [ ] Write down what the top three objections actually were, and fold them into
      `objections.md` and the FAQ.
- [ ] Check the analytics funnel: sessions → `/pricing` → checkout start → paid. If HN sent 5,000
      sessions and no purchases, the problem is the pricing page or the trial, not the traffic.
- [ ] Reply to anything still open. A thread answered a day late still reads as answered.

## If it flops

It might. Most Show HNs get twelve points and vanish, and that is not a verdict on the product.
The pages, the clips and the objection scripts all still exist afterwards, and they are what the
SEO work compounds on. Do not relaunch the same thing in a month with a new title; ship something
that is genuinely different first — macOS, a trial, or a feature worth its own post.
